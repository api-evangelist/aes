The AES Corporation is a Fortune 500 global energy company that generates and distributes electrical power. AES operates a diverse portfolio of renewable, thermal, LNG, and battery storage facilities across multiple countries, serving utilities, industrial facilities, and end users. AES is the largest global supplier of clean energy to corporations in the Americas and operates 34.7 GW globally with $12.2 billion in annual revenue. The company serves 18.7 million household equivalents through utilities in Indiana, Ohio, and El Salvador. AES also co-founded Fluence Energy, a leading energy storage and software platform company.
