# AES Corporation

> The AES Corporation (NYSE: AES) is a Fortune 500 global power company that
> generates and distributes electricity. AES runs 34.7 GW of renewable, thermal,
> LNG and battery-storage capacity across the Americas and beyond, serves 18.7
> million household equivalents through regulated utilities in Indiana, Ohio and
> El Salvador, and is the largest supplier of clean energy to corporations in the
> Americas. AES co-founded Fluence Energy with Siemens.

## Products (enterprise, no public API)

- [Fluence Mosaic](https://fluenceenergy.com/software/mosaic/): AI bidding
  application that automates wholesale-market bidding for battery storage.
- [Fluence Nispera](https://fluenceenergy.com/software/nispera/): asset performance
  management for solar, wind and storage portfolios.
- [Fluence IQ Platform](https://fluenceenergy.com/fluence-iq-digital-platform/):
  the cloud platform both applications sit on. Fluence describes internal APIs; none
  are publicly documented.
